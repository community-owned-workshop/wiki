# 📦 I Have a Mod

**Welcome!**

COW helps mod authors keep their projects alive without carrying the entire maintenance burden alone.

You stay the author.

The community helps with maintenance.


## Why join?

- Accept community bug fixes.
- Share maintenance work.
- Use automated testing and publishing.
- Keep one official Workshop page.
- Avoid dozens of competing forks.


## What changes?

Almost nothing.

Your repository stays yours.

The only difference is that trusted contributors can help maintain it through Pull Requests.


## Getting Started

1. Move your project into the COW organization. <br>**OR**<br>Create a new one from our template.
2. Enable the COW GitHub Actions.
3. Invite contributors.
4. **Keep creating awesome mods!**
